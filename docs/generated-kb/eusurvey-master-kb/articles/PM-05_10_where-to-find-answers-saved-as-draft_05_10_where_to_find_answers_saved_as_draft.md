## Intent / Description

Explain how participants can retrieve answers saved as draft.

## Short Answer

After selecting Save as draft, EUSurvey redirects you to a page showing a link that lets you retrieve your draft to edit and submit your answers.

## Prerequisites / Required Permissions

Not specified in source document.

## Procedure

1. Select Save as draft while answering the survey.
2. After redirection, copy or save the draft retrieval link displayed on the page.
3. Save the link by email, browser favourites, or clipboard.
4. Use the saved link later to retrieve, edit, and submit your draft answers.

## Important Conditions / Limitations

* You must save the draft retrieval link.
* Without the draft link, you may not be able to retrieve the saved draft.
