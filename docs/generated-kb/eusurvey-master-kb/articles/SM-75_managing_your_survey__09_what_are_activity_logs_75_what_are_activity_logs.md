## Short Answer

Activity logs monitor and log the activity on your survey. This way, you can check which user applied which change to your survey and at what time. You can also export the activity logs into several file formats such as xls, csv and ods. Enter the activity log of your survey by clicking on the 'Activity' page, next to 'Properties'. If the activity logs are empty, it may be that they are deactivated system-wide. Find here (https://ec.europa.eu/eusurvey/resources/documents/ActivityLogEvents.xlsx) a list of the logged events.
