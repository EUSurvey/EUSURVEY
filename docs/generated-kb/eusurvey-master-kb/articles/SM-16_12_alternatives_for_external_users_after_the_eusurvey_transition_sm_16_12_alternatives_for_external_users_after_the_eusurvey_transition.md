## Short Answer

External users can work with an EU institution, use another survey platform, or deploy their own EUSurvey instance under the European Union Public Licence.

## Important Conditions / Limitations

* An EU official can create the survey and grant Form Manager privileges.
* External users may choose another commercial or open-source survey platform that meets their requirements.
* Organizations can deploy their own EUSurvey instance from the open-source code and host it on their infrastructure.
