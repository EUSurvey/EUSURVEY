## Short Answer

External users should export their survey data before April 2027. The exported files can be used for reporting, analysis, or archiving.

## Prerequisites / Required Permissions

Not specified in source document.

## Procedure

1. Log in to EUSurvey.
2. Open the survey.
3. Go to the Results tab in the tabular view.
4. Click Export.
5. Enter a name for the export file.
6. Select a format, for example XLSX.
7. Download the file from the Exports tab once it is ready.
