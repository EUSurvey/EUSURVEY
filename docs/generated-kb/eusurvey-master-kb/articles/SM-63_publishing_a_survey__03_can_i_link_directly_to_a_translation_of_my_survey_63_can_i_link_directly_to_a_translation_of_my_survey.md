# SM-63_publishing_a_survey__03_can_i_link_directly_to_a_translation_of_my_survey — 63. Can I link directly to a translation of my survey?

## Intent / Description

This article helps EUSurvey survey managers understand or perform the task: Can I link directly to a translation of my survey.

## Applies To

* EUSurvey area: Publishing a survey
* Environment: All

* UI location: Not specified
* Backend location: Not specified

## Short Answer

When you send out invitations containing the link or use the link to the published form on the 'Overview' page, by default the link points to the form in the main language.

## Prerequisites / Required Permissions

Not specified in source document.

## Procedure

1. You can also redirect respondents directly to a specific translation by using this link:
2. https://ec.europa.eu/eusurvey/runner/SurveyAlias?surveylanguage=LC
3. Just replace:
4. SurveyAlias with the alias of your survey LC with the appropriate ISO 639-1 language code (e.g. FR for French, DE for German, etc.)
