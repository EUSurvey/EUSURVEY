## Short Answer

You can archive your survey with all its submitted answers to reload it or to launch the same survey again at a later point in time. To archive your survey, select the 'Archive Survey' icon in the action menu of the 'Overview' page.

## Prerequisites / Required Permissions

Not specified in source document.

## Procedure

1. Archived surveys cannot be edited or collect any data. But you can export results or request a PDF-file of your survey. The archived survey will be available from the 'Dashboard' page, from where it can also be restored. A restored survey can be edited again.
