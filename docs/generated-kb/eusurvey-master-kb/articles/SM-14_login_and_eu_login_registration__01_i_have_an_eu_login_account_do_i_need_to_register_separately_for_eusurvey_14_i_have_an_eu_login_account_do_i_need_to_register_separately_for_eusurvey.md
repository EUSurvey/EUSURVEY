## Short Answer

No, there is no need to register separately to EUSurvey. An EU Login account is enough. You can access EUSurvey by clicking on the login button on the EUSurvey homepage (https://ec.europa.eu/eusurvey/home/welcome) . This takes you to the login screen (see below for more details).
