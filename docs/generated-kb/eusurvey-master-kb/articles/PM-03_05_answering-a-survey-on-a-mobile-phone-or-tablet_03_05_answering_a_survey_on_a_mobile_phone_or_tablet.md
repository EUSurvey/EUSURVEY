# PM-03_05_answering-a-survey-on-a-mobile-phone-or-tablet — 03.05 - Answering a survey on a mobile phone or tablet

## Intent / Description

Explain whether EUSurvey questionnaires can be answered on mobile devices.

## Applies To

* Role(s): Respondent
* EUSurvey area: Accessing a survey
* Environment: All
* Article type: Troubleshooting
* UI location: Not specified
* Backend location: Not specified

## Short Answer

Yes. EUSurvey has a responsive design for published surveys, so the questionnaire adapts to the screen size and resolution of the device you use.

## Prerequisites / Required Permissions

Not specified in source document.

## Procedure

1. Open the survey link on your mobile phone or tablet.
2. Answer the questionnaire as displayed on your device.
3. If the layout does not work well, try using a supported desktop browser.

## Important Conditions / Limitations

* The published survey design adapts to different screen sizes and resolutions.
* Some surveys may contain complex questions or layouts that are easier to answer on a larger screen.
