## Short Answer

You can export two different things in EUSurvey: the responses collected for a survey, and the survey questionnaire itself. Survey responses are exported from the Results tab and can be used for analysis or archiving. The questionnaire and its settings are exported from the Surveys page or Overview page as an .eus file.

## Procedure

1. Export survey responses
2. Open the survey.
3. Go to the Results tab, in the tabular view.
4. Click Export.
5. Enter a name for the export file.
6. Select the required export format, for example XLSX.
7. Wait for the notification that the export file is ready.
8. Go to the Exports tab.
9. Download the generated file.
10. Export the survey questionnaire
11. Go to the Surveys page.
12. Search for the survey you want to export.
13. Click the Export icon next to the survey.
14. Alternatively, open the survey, go to the Overview page, and click the Export icon there.
15. Save the exported file to your computer. The survey questionnaire and settings are exported as a file with the .eus extension.

## Important Conditions / Limitations

* The response export reflects the filters and question-display settings applied when the export is generated.
* The .eus export contains the survey questionnaire and settings, not the submitted responses.
* Use the response export for analysis, reporting, or archiving of collected data.
* Use the .eus export to back up the questionnaire or transfer the survey structure to another EUSurvey instance.
* When exported data contains sensitive information, store and protect the exported file appropriately.
