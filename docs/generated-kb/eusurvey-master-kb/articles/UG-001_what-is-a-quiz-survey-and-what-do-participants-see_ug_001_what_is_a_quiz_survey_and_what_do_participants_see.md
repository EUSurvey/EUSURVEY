## Short Answer

A quiz is a special EUSurvey survey type that calculates a final score for each participant. Participants see a quiz start page, complete a questionnaire that looks like a normal survey, and then receive a quiz results page showing their score, maximum score, score chart, answers, correctness, points and a PDF download option.

## Important Conditions / Limitations

* The quiz questionnaire itself looks like a normal questionnaire.
* The quiz results page can display the final score, maximum score, per-question scores, correct/incorrect indicators, points and feedback.
