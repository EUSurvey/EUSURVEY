## Intent / Description

Define the contribution ID and explain why it is useful.

## Short Answer

The contribution ID is the individual number automatically generated for your submitted answer. It is a unique identifier that allows EUSurvey to recognise your contribution.

## Prerequisites / Required Permissions

Not specified in source document.

## Procedure

1. After submitting your contribution, check the Confirmation page.
2. Note or save the contribution ID displayed there.
3. If you save or receive a PDF copy, check the upper-left side of the PDF for the contribution ID.
4. Use the contribution ID when contacting EUSurvey support or the survey organisers about your contribution.

## Important Conditions / Limitations

* The contribution ID is unique to your contribution.
* It is useful for support requests and for actions related to your submitted answers.
