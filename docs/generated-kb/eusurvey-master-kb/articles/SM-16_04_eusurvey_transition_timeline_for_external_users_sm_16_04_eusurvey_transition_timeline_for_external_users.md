## Short Answer

The main transition milestones are 31 August 2026 for the restriction on new survey creation by external users, September 2026 to March 2027 for migration and support, 1 April 2027 for read-only mode, and 1 September 2027 for final closure.

## Important Conditions / Limitations

* During read-only mode, surveys cannot be edited and no new responses can be collected.
* Survey data remains available for viewing and export until final closure.
* Users should export data or transfer ownership before the applicable deadlines.
