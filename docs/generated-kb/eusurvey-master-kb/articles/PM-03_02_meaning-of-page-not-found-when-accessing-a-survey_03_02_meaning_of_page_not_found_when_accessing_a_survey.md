# PM-03_02_meaning-of-page-not-found-when-accessing-a-survey — 03.02 - Meaning of Page not found when accessing a survey

## Intent / Description

Explain why a participant may see Page not found when opening a survey link.

## Applies To

* Role(s): Respondent
* EUSurvey area: Accessing a survey
* Environment: All
* Article type: Troubleshooting
* UI location: Not specified
* Backend location: Not specified

## Short Answer

Page not found usually means that the link used to access the survey is incorrect, or that the survey has already been removed from EUSurvey.

## Prerequisites / Required Permissions

Not specified in source document.

## Procedure

1. Check whether the survey link was copied completely and correctly.
2. If you think the link is valid, inform the author of the survey directly.
3. If the link was published by another body or website, inform the body responsible for publishing the link that it is incorrect.

## Important Conditions / Limitations

* This article applies when the survey page itself cannot be found.
* If the survey exists but you are not authorised to access it, a different access-related message may be displayed.
