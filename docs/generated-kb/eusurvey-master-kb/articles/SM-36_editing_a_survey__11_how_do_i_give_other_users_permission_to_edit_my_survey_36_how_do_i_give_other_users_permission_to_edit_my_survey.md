## Intent / Description

This article helps EUSurvey survey managers understand or perform the task: How do I give other users permission to edit my survey.

## Short Answer

Open your survey and open the 'Privileges' page. Click on 'Add new user' or 'Add department'. A wizard will pop up that guides you through the process of adding users. Next you can give them specific access rights. Simply click on the colour to change the rights.

## Procedure

1. Green: Read and Write access Yellow: Read access Red: No access
2. Added users will automatically see your survey in their list of surveys next time they log into EUSurvey.
3. External owners or survey organizers cannot see EU domains on the Privileges tab / Add users button, therefore, they cannot provide access for these persons directly. Please contact us (https://ec.europa.eu/eusurvey/home/support) to request privileges for external users.
4. Read more about this under 'How do I give other users access to my survey?'
