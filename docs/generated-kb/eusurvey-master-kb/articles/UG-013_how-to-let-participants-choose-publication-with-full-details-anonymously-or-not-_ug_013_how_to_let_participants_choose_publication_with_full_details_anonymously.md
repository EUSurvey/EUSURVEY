# UG-013_how-to-let-participants-choose-publication-with-full-details-anonymously-or-not- — UG-013 — How to let participants choose publication with full details, anonymously, or not at all

## Intent / Description

Describe a questionnaire design that lets participants choose different publication levels.

## Applies To

* Role(s): Survey Owner
* EUSurvey area: Users Guides

## Short Answer

Use a single choice question with options such as With full personal detail, Anonymous, and Not at all. Use dependencies to show separate personal-information fields for each option, then configure published results to include only the questions and contribution options that should appear publicly.

## Important Conditions / Limitations

* Use distinct identifiers for duplicated free text questions so that the fields can be selected accurately in publication settings.
* This design can collect personal information while respecting the participant’s publication choice.
