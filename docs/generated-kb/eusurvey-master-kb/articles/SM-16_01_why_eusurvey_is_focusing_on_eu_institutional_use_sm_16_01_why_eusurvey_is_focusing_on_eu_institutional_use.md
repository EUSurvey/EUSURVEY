## Short Answer

EUSurvey is developed and maintained by the European Commission primarily to support EU Institutions, Bodies, Offices and Agencies. The transition supports long-term sustainability, EU governance, security, data protection compliance, and reliable support for institutional users.

## Important Conditions / Limitations

* This change does not prevent external users from responding to surveys.

* External users can still export data during the transition period.
