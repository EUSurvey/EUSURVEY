## Short Answer

A survey can be transferred to an EU institution using the Change Owner feature. After transfer, the survey continues to function under institutional management.

## Prerequisites / Required Permissions

Not specified in source document.

## Procedure

1. Identify a contact in the relevant EU institution.
2. Use the Change Owner option in EUSurvey.
3. Transfer the survey to the institutional account.
4. After transfer, ensure the external user has the appropriate permissions if they still need to manage the survey.
