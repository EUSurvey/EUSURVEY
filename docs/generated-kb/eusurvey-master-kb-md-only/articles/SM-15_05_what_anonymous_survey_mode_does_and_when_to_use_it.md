## Short Answer

When Anonymous survey mode is activated, contributions are recorded anonymously. EUSurvey does not collect or store user-related data for those contributions, including IP addresses. To keep the survey fully anonymous, the questionnaire itself must also avoid asking respondents for personal data.

## Procedure

1. Activate anonymous survey mode
2. Open the survey.
3. Go to Survey Properties.
4. Open the Security section.
5. Enable Anonymous survey mode.
6. Review the survey questions to ensure that they do not request personal data if the survey is intended to be fully anonymous.

## Important Conditions / Limitations

* Do not include questions asking for names, email addresses, phone numbers, identification numbers or other personal data if the survey must be fully anonymous.
* Once the survey has been launched and at least one response has been collected, anonymous survey mode cannot be deactivated.
* It is not possible to revert the survey to non-anonymous mode after responses have been submitted.
* Anonymous mode does not anonymize personal data that the survey manager explicitly asks respondents to provide in the questionnaire.
