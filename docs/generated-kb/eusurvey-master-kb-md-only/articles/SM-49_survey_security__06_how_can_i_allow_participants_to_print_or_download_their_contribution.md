## Short Answer

## Prerequisites / Required Permissions

Not specified in source document.

## Procedure

1. To activate this feature, follow the steps below:
2. 1. Open your survey 2. Go to 'Properties' tab 3. Go to 'Security' tab 4. Enable 'Allows participants to print their contribution and receive it as a PDF' option
