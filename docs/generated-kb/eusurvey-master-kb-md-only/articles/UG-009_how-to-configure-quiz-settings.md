## Intent / Description

Describe the quiz-level settings available in survey properties.

## Short Answer

Quiz settings are configured from the survey Properties page under Quiz Settings. They control whether quiz functionality is enabled, whether participants see their total score or scores by question, and the messages shown on the quiz start and results pages.

## Important Conditions / Limitations

* If Total Score is set to No, participants do not see their final score on the quiz results page.
* If Scores by question is set to Yes, participants see points and feedback for individual questions.
