## Short Answer

To enable Email Reports, open the survey settings, activate the Automatic report per email switch, choose the report frequency, and add recipients.

## Prerequisites / Required Permissions

Not specified in source document.

## Procedure

1. Configure Email Reports
2. Go to the survey settings.
3. Find the Automatic report per email switch.
4. Turn the switch on.
5. Select the frequency: Never, Daily, Weekly or Monthly.
6. Enter one or more recipient email addresses separated by semicolons.
7. Click Add to confirm the recipients.
8. Save the settings.
