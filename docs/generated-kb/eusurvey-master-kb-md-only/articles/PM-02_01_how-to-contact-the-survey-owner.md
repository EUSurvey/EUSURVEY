# PM-02_01_how-to-contact-the-survey-owner — 02.01 - How to contact the survey owner

## Intent / Description

Explain how participants can contact the owner or organiser of a survey.

## Applies To

* Role(s): Respondent
* EUSurvey area: Contacting the survey owner
* Environment: All

## Short Answer

You can contact the survey owner using the Contact option on the right-side panel of the survey page.

## Prerequisites / Required Permissions

Not specified in source document.

## Procedure

1. Open the survey page.
2. Look for the right-side panel.
3. Select the Contact option.
4. Use the available contact information or form to reach the survey owner.

## Important Conditions / Limitations

* The survey owner is responsible for survey-specific questions, deadlines, access, and content.
* If the contact option is not available and your issue is technical, use the EUSurvey support form where appropriate.
