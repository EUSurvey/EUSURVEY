# PM-05_01_view-or-print-a-contribution-after-submission — 05.01 - View or print a contribution after submission

## Intent / Description

Explain how participants can view or print their contribution immediately after submitting it.

## Applies To

* Role(s): Respondent
* EUSurvey area: After contributing
* Environment: All
* Article type: Troubleshooting
* UI location: Not specified
* Backend location: Not specified

## Short Answer

Yes. Right after submission, EUSurvey offers a print option on the Confirmation page.

## Prerequisites / Required Permissions

Not specified in source document.

## Procedure

1. Submit your contribution.
2. Stay on the Confirmation page.
3. Use the print option offered on that page.

## Important Conditions / Limitations

* The print option is available immediately after submission.
* If you leave the Confirmation page without printing or saving a copy, you may need your contribution ID for later actions.
