# PM-03_03_meaning-of-this-survey-has-not-yet-been-published-or-has-already-been- — 03.03 - Meaning of This survey has not yet been published or has already been unpublished

## Intent / Description

Explain why participants may see a message saying a survey is not published or has been unpublished.

## Applies To

* Role(s): Respondent
* EUSurvey area: Accessing a survey
* Environment: All
* Article type: Troubleshooting
* UI location: Not specified
* Backend location: Not specified

## Short Answer

This message usually means that the invitation was sent, but the survey has not yet been published, or that it has already been unpublished by the survey organisers.

## Prerequisites / Required Permissions

Not specified in source document.

## Procedure

1. Wait and try again if the survey may not yet have been opened.
2. If the survey should already be available, contact the survey owner for further information.

## Important Conditions / Limitations

* The availability of a survey is controlled by the survey organisers.
* EUSurvey support may not be able to change the publication status of a survey owned by another organiser.
