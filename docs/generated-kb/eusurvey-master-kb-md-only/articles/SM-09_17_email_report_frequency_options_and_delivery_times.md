## Short Answer

Email Reports can be sent daily, weekly or monthly. Daily reports are sent between 00:00 and 07:00 and cover the previous day. Weekly reports are sent every Monday between 00:00 and 07:00 and cover the previous Monday to Sunday. Monthly reports are sent on the first day of each month and cover the previous month.

## Important Conditions / Limitations

* After selecting a frequency, EUSurvey remembers the choice.
* The first daily report arrives the next day.
* The first weekly report arrives the following Monday.
* The first monthly report arrives on the first day of the next month.
