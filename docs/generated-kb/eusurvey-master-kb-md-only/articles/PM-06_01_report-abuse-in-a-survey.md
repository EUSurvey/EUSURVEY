# PM-06_01_report-abuse-in-a-survey — 06.01 - Report abuse in a survey

## Intent / Description

Explain how participants can report a survey that contains illegal content or violates rights.

## Applies To

* Role(s): Respondent
* EUSurvey area: Report abuse
* Environment: All
* Article type: Troubleshooting
* UI location: Not specified
* Backend location: Not specified

## Short Answer

If a survey contains illegal content or violates the rights of others, use the Report Abuse link on the right-side panel of the survey.

## Prerequisites / Required Permissions

Not specified in source document.

## Procedure

1. Open the survey page.
2. Look for the right-side panel.
3. Select Report Abuse.
4. Follow the reporting process.
5. Refer to the EUSurvey Terms of Service for more information: https://ec.europa.eu/eusurvey/home/tos.

## Important Conditions / Limitations

* This applies to illegal content or content that violates rights, including intellectual property rights, competition law, or general law.
* Use the survey owner contact option for ordinary survey-specific questions.
