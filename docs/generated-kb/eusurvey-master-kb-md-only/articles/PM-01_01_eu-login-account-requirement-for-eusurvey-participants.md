# PM-01_01_eu-login-account-requirement-for-eusurvey-participants — 01.01 - EU Login account requirement for EUSurvey participants

## Intent / Description

Explain whether participants who already have an EU Login account need a separate EUSurvey registration.

## Applies To

* Role(s): Respondent
* EUSurvey area: Logging in and creating an account
* Environment: All
* Article type: Troubleshooting
* UI location: Not specified
* Backend location: Not specified

## Short Answer

No. An EU Login account is enough to access EUSurvey. You do not need to register separately for EUSurvey if you already have an EU Login account.

## Prerequisites / Required Permissions

Not specified in source document.

## Procedure

1. Open the EUSurvey homepage: https://ec.europa.eu/eusurvey/home/welcome.
2. Select the Login button.
3. Sign in using your EU Login account.

## Important Conditions / Limitations

* This article applies to participants/respondents accessing EUSurvey.
* This article does not explain how to create an EU Login account from scratch.
