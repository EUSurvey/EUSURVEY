## Short Answer

Please click on the login button on the EUSurvey homepage (https://ec.europa.eu/eusurvey/home/welcome) . You will be redirected to the EUSurvey login screen.

## Prerequisites / Required Permissions

Not specified in source document.

## Procedure

1. Once you arrived on the login screen, you have to choose the option corresponding to your personal case: If you work for an EU institution, choose the second option to connect to the EUSurvey application. Your EU Login username and password will then be sufficient. If you don't work for an EU institution (Externals), choose the first option to connect to the EUSurvey application. You will need to have previously registered your mobile phone to pass the two-factor authentication (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help:Two-factor_authentication).
2. If you don't have an EU Login account, please create one by clicking here (https://webgate.ec.europa.eu/cas/eim/external/register.cgi) .
3. In case you don't work for an EU institution, please also register your mobile phone by clicking here (https://ecas.ec.europa.eu/cas/userdata/mobileApp/manageMyMobileDevices.cgi) .
