## Short Answer

You are encouraged to contact DGT before you finalise your survey. Its Editing unit (email: DGT-EDIT) will check that your survey is clearly drafted and presented. For more info, see DGT's MyIntraComm pages (https://myintracomm.ec.europa.eu/serv/en/dgt/Pages/index.aspx) .

## Procedure

1. You can also get your survey translated into the official EU languages by DGT. First export it as an XML file and send it via Poetry with the requester code of the DG concerned. It should be a maximum of 15 000 characters, excl. spaces (Word count).
