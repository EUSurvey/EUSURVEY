## Short Answer

Email Report recipients must be entered as valid email addresses. EUSurvey validates the format and checks that the domain is not on a known blacklist. A maximum of 10 email addresses is allowed.

## Important Conditions / Limitations

* Separate multiple email addresses with a semicolon.
* Each email appears on a separate line after it is added.
* Remove an email address by clicking the cross next to it.
* The input field has a 255-character limit.
* If an email address is invalid, an error message is shown.
