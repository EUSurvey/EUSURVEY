# PM-05_07_what-to-do-if-a-contribution-was-not-submitted-on-time — 05.07 - What to do if a contribution was not submitted on time

## Intent / Description

Explain what participants should do when they miss a submission deadline or their contribution is not submitted in time.

## Applies To

* Role(s): Respondent
* EUSurvey area: After contributing
* Environment: All
* Article type: Troubleshooting
* UI location: Not specified
* Backend location: Not specified

## Short Answer

Contact the survey organisers directly. If the survey organisers’ contact details are not available on the survey page, contact the EUSurvey support team.

## Prerequisites / Required Permissions

Not specified in source document.

## Procedure

1. Check the survey page for contact details of the survey organisers.
2. Contact the survey organisers and explain that your contribution was not submitted on time.
3. If no organiser contact details are available, contact EUSurvey support: https://ec.europa.eu/eusurvey/home/support.

## Important Conditions / Limitations

* Survey deadlines and acceptance of late contributions are managed by the survey organisers.
* EUSurvey support can help with technical issues but may not be able to change survey-specific deadlines.
