## Short Answer

Keep in mind that the target group of participants can display the survey easily if the font you used is installed in their browser. UTF-8 is the most common encoding for the HTML pages. On the other hand, choosing non-supported fonts may affect the rendering of PDF export.

3. 'Freesans' is the default font used
4. In case of doubt, run a PDF export of your final survey to check if your survey is rendered correctly in PDF. Beware however that some contributions may not to be rendered correctly in PDF. Your participants, indeed, are free to choose any font among those which are supported by the application. Even though the application is unable to render the characters they have used, these are saved correctly on the EUSurvey's database. Thus they can be exported from the results page.
