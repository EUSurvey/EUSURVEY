# How do I report a spam or abusive survey on EUSurvey?

## Intent / Description

Explains how any user can report a survey that contains spam, illegal content, or abusive material.

## Applies To

* Role(s): Respondent, Any user
* Feature: Report Abuse
* Context: While viewing or completing a survey

## Short Answer

If you encounter a survey that contains spam, illegal content, propaganda, hate speech, inappropriate images, or promotional abuse, you can report it directly from the survey page using the **Report Abuse** link. EUSurvey reviews all reports and may take action including removing the survey.

## Steps / Procedure

1. Open the survey in your browser.
2. Look for the **Report Abuse** link on the right-side panel of the survey page.
3. Click **Report Abuse**.
4. Select the type of abuse from the available options:
   - **Fake content** – misleading or fraudulent survey
   - **Propaganda** – political propaganda or manipulation
   - **Hate speech** – content inciting hatred or discrimination
   - **Inappropriate images** – offensive visual content
   - **Promotional abuse** – unsolicited advertising or spam
   - **Others** – any other type of abuse (describe in the text field)
5. Optionally provide additional details in the text field.
6. Optionally provide your email address (not required).
7. Complete the captcha verification.
8. Submit the report.

## Important Conditions / Limitations

* You do not need an EU Login account to report abuse.
* The report is sent to the EUSurvey administration team, not to the survey owner.
* A captcha must be completed to prevent automated submissions.
* You will see a confirmation message once the report is submitted successfully.
* You cannot track the status of your report after submission.
* The Report Abuse link is only available on the survey page itself — you cannot report a survey from the EUSurvey homepage.

## Troubleshooting / Related Cases

* If you cannot see the Report Abuse link, the survey may be embedded in an external website. Try accessing the survey directly at its EUSurvey URL.
* If you want to contact the survey creator about a question or issue with the survey content (not abuse), use the "Contact" link instead.

## Out of Scope / Separate Topics

* Reporting phishing emails pretending to come from EUSurvey (see: KB-EUSURVEY-ABUSE-002)
* Contacting the survey owner about survey content questions
* Reporting technical problems with EUSurvey
