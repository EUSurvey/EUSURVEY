# PM-05_06_seeing-other-peoples-answers-after-contributing — 05.06 - Seeing other peoples answers after contributing

## Intent / Description

Explain whether participants can see other contributions or published results after submitting.

## Applies To

* Role(s): Respondent
* EUSurvey area: After contributing
* Environment: All
* Article type: Troubleshooting
* UI location: Not specified
* Backend location: Not specified

## Short Answer

It depends on the survey settings. If you do not see a link to published results after submitting your contribution, this feature may not be available for that survey.

## Prerequisites / Required Permissions

Not specified in source document.

## Procedure

1. Submit your contribution.
2. Check whether the Confirmation page or survey page provides a link to published results.
3. If no link is available and you believe the results should be public, contact the survey author.

## Important Conditions / Limitations

* The possibility to see other answers is controlled by the survey settings.
* Published results may not be available for all surveys.
