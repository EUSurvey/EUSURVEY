## Short Answer

EU-funded projects are not automatically exempt. If a survey is owned by an external user, it follows the same transition timeline. To continue using EUSurvey beyond the transition period, the survey should be transferred to an EU institutional account or created under EU institutional ownership with appropriate permissions for external project users.
