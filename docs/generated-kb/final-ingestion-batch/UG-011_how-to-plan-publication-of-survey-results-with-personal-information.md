# UG-011_how-to-plan-publication-of-survey-results-with-personal-information — UG-011 — How to plan publication of survey results with personal information

## Intent / Description

Help survey managers decide what participant data and contribution data can be published.

## Applies To

* Role(s): Survey Owner
* EUSurvey area: Users Guides
* Environment: All
* Article type: Guidance
* UI location: Not specified

## Short Answer

Before publishing results, decide what information should be published, which contributions should be published, and what privacy statement applies. Participants should be informed how their personal data will be handled.

## Important Conditions / Limitations

* The privacy statement is especially important when personal information is collected.
* Do not rely only on technical filtering; the questionnaire design must match the publication intention.
