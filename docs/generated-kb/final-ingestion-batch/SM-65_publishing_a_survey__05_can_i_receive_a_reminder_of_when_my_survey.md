## Intent / Description

This article helps EUSurvey survey managers understand or perform the task: Can I receive a reminder of when my survey will end.

## Short Answer

EUSurvey can send you an email reminder when your survey is about to end. This allows you to prepare the next steps (e.g. organising resources for result analysis).

## Prerequisites / Required Permissions

Not specified in source document.

## Procedure

1. To enable this option, open the survey and go to the 'Properties' page. Select 'Advanced Settings' section and, click on the 'Edit' button and enable 'End Notification', specifying how long in advance you expect an email. Click on 'Save'. The reminder email will be delivered to all form managers
