## Procedure

1. Please note that your participants may not want to take part if you cannot guarantee the confidentiality of the data submitted.
2. For EU staff only:
3. We draw your attention to the policy on the "protection of natural persons with regard to the processing of personal data Regulation (EU) 2018/1725 (https://eur- lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=uriserv:OJ.L_.2018.295.01.0039.01.ENG&toc=OJ:L:2018:295:TOC) .
4. If personal data is collected, a privacy statement must be drafted and then published together with the questionnaire.
5. Please contact the DPC (Data Protection Coordinator) of your DG in order to validate the privacy statement.
6. Additionally, any collection of personal data must be notified to the Data Protection Officer (DPO). Please contact your DPC if you need assistance for notification to the DPO.
7. Please find below a privacy statement template that you could use for your surveys. You must change and adapt it to your needs:
8. Template: 'Privacy Statement template for surveys and consultations' (https://circabc.europa.eu/ui/group/599f39d2-e0cc-4765-bfdc- c9917c931509/library/dfed4f34-fa25-42ed-af44-e1acc4f0a58f/details)
