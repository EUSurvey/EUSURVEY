## Short Answer

Existing surveys created by external users remain available during the transition period. In April 2027, they switch to read-only mode. In September 2027, external surveys are permanently closed and archived.

## Important Conditions / Limitations

* In read-only mode, surveys cannot be edited and no new responses can be collected.
* Users should export their data before the read-only phase begins.
* Surveys created by EU Institutions, Bodies, Offices or Agencies are not affected.
