## Short Answer

When a survey has not received new contributions for one year, EUSurvey starts a notification process before deletion. If the survey remains inactive after the notifications, it is automatically deleted from the system.

## Important Conditions / Limitations

* First notification: the survey owner and privileged management users are informed that the survey is inactive and subject to deletion.
* Second notification: a reminder is sent if no action is taken.
* Final notification: a last notification is sent before deletion.
* The timing may change through configuration, but the initial schedule is 60 days, 45 days and 3 days before deletion.
