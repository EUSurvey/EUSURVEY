# Can a malicious or cloned survey be removed from EUSurvey?

## Intent / Description

Explains whether and how a fraudulent, cloned, or malicious survey can be removed from the platform.

## Applies To

* Role(s): Any user, Survey manager (victim of cloning)
* Feature: Report Abuse, Administration
* Context: A user encounters a survey that impersonates another organisation or survey

## Short Answer

Yes. If a survey is confirmed as malicious, fraudulent, or a clone designed to impersonate another organisation's legitimate survey, the EUSurvey administration team can freeze, unpublish, or delete it after reviewing an abuse report. Only the EUSurvey team can remove a survey that belongs to another user — you cannot directly take down someone else's survey.

## Steps / Procedure

1. Report the malicious survey using the **Report Abuse** link on the survey page.
2. Select "Fake content" as the abuse type (or "Others" if the situation is more complex).
3. Provide as much detail as possible: why you believe the survey is fraudulent, which legitimate survey it imitates, and any evidence of malicious intent.
4. Provide your email address so the team can follow up if needed.
5. Submit the report.
6. The EUSurvey administration team will review the report and may take one or more of the following actions:
   - **Freeze** the survey (prevent further submissions)
   - **Unpublish** the survey (make it inaccessible to respondents)
   - **Delete** the survey (permanently remove it)
   - **Ban** the survey owner's account

## Important Conditions / Limitations

* Only the EUSurvey administration team can freeze, unpublish, or delete a survey created by another user.
* The survey owner cannot override a freeze or ban imposed by administrators.
* Surveys marked as frozen cannot be edited, published, or accept new contributions.
* A banned user loses access to all their surveys and cannot create new ones.
* There is no guaranteed timeline for abuse report processing — it depends on the severity and volume of reports.

## Troubleshooting / Related Cases

* If the fraudulent survey is collecting sensitive personal data, mention this urgency in the report text.
* If you are the legitimate survey owner whose survey was cloned, include your own survey URL for comparison.
