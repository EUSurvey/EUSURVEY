# PM-05_09_little-boxes-shown-on-the-pdf-export-of-a-survey — 05.09 - Little boxes shown on the PDF export of a survey

## Intent / Description

Explain why unsupported characters may appear as little boxes in a PDF export.

## Applies To

* Role(s): Respondent
* EUSurvey area: After contributing
* Environment: All
* Article type: Troubleshooting
* UI location: Not specified
* Backend location: Not specified

## Short Answer

Little boxes may appear when fonts used by the survey authors or participants are not supported by the application. If a character cannot be found on the system, the PDF rendering engine replaces it with a little box.

## Prerequisites / Required Permissions

Not specified in source document.

## Procedure

1. Check whether the issue concerns the PDF display only.
2. Report the unsupported character to the contact information shown in the survey information area.
3. Keep in mind that the saved contribution itself is not affected if it was submitted correctly.

## Important Conditions / Limitations

* This does not affect your contribution once it has been saved correctly.
* The responsible authority for the survey can still visualise and export the contribution even if the PDF rendering engine could not render the PDF correctly.
