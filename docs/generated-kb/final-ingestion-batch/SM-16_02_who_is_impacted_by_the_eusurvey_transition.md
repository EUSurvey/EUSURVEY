## Short Answer

The change affects external users with external EU Login accounts who create or manage surveys in the EUSurvey back office. From 31 August 2026, these users will no longer be able to create new surveys themselves unless the survey is created under EU institutional responsibility.

## Important Conditions / Limitations

* Examples of impacted users include citizens, businesses, consultants, researchers, NGOs, national public administrations and Member State authorities.
* Existing surveys created by external users remain accessible during the transition period.
