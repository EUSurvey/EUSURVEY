# How a respondent can reopen a saved draft contribution

## Intent / Description

Explains how a respondent can return to a contribution that was saved as a draft, including what link or information is required and what conditions apply.

## Applies To

* Role(s): Respondent
* Feature: Save as draft, Draft continuation
* Context: A respondent previously started answering a survey, saved their progress, and now wants to continue

## Short Answer

When a respondent saves their answers as a draft, they receive a **draft link** (unique URL) that allows them to return and continue their contribution later. This link is shown on-screen after saving and may also be sent by email if the respondent provides their email address during the save process.

Without this draft link, the respondent cannot resume their saved draft unless they were using an invitation link (in which case the draft is linked to the invitation).

## Steps / Procedure

**To resume a saved draft:**

1. Open the **draft link** that was provided when you saved. This is a unique URL containing your draft identifier.
2. The survey will open with your previously saved answers pre-filled.
3. Continue answering and either save again or submit.

**If you used an invitation link:**

1. Click the same invitation link from your invitation email.
2. If a draft was saved for that invitation, you will be taken to your saved draft automatically.

**If you saved via the "Save as Draft" button and provided your email:**

1. Check your email for a message containing the draft continuation link.
2. Click the link to resume.

## Important Conditions / Limitations

* **Draft link required**: Without the draft link, there is no way to resume the draft. EUSurvey does not provide an account-based list of drafts for anonymous respondents.
* **Save as Draft must be enabled**: The survey owner must have enabled the "Save as Draft" feature in the survey's security settings. Not all surveys support this.
* **Survey must still be published**: If the survey has been unpublished, closed (end date passed), or deleted, the draft link will no longer work.
* **No automatic expiry of drafts**: Saved drafts remain available as long as the survey is active, unless the survey owner deletes old drafts or the system's automatic cleanup removes very old drafts.
* **Invitation-linked drafts**: When a participant with an invitation saves a draft, the draft is associated with their invitation. Returning via the invitation link automatically resumes the draft.
* **Difference from submitted contribution**: A draft is not visible in the Results. It becomes a contribution only after final submission.
* **Authentication-based access**: If the survey requires EU Login authentication, the respondent must log in before the draft can be resumed.

## Troubleshooting / Related Cases

* If the draft link no longer works: the survey may have been unpublished or closed. Contact the survey owner.
* If you lost the draft link and did not provide an email: the draft cannot be recovered. You must start a new contribution.
* If you lost the draft link but used an invitation: try clicking the original invitation link again.
* If the saved draft shows old answers and the survey has changed: the survey owner may have applied changes after you saved. Your answers to unchanged questions should persist.
