# What abuse types can I report on EUSurvey?

## Intent / Description

Lists the specific types of abuse that can be reported through the EUSurvey Report Abuse form and what each category means.

## Applies To

* Role(s): Respondent, Any user
* Feature: Report Abuse
* Context: Choosing the right category when reporting survey abuse

## Short Answer

When reporting a survey through the Report Abuse form, you must select one of six predefined abuse categories. Choose the category that best matches the problematic content:

- **Fake content** – The survey is misleading, impersonates another organisation, or collects data under false pretenses.
- **Propaganda** – The survey promotes political propaganda or attempts to manipulate opinions.
- **Hate speech** – The survey contains content inciting hatred or discrimination against individuals or groups.
- **Inappropriate images** – The survey displays offensive, violent, or sexually explicit visual content.
- **Promotional abuse** – The survey is used for unsolicited advertising, spam, or commercial promotion unrelated to legitimate consultation.
- **Others** – Any other type of abuse not covered above (you can describe it in the text field).

## Steps / Procedure

1. Access the survey and click **Report Abuse** on the right-side panel.
2. Select the most appropriate category from the list above.
3. If you select "Others", describe the issue in the text field.
4. Optionally provide your email address if you would like to be contacted.
5. Complete the captcha and submit.

## Important Conditions / Limitations

* You can only select one abuse type per report.
* If you want to report multiple issues, submit multiple reports or use "Others" with a detailed description.
* Reports are processed by the EUSurvey administration team.
* The team decides whether to freeze, unpublish, or delete the reported survey.

## Troubleshooting / Related Cases

* If you are not sure which category to choose, select "Others" and describe the issue.
* Reporting abuse does not immediately remove the survey — it triggers an internal review.
