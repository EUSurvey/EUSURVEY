## Procedure

1. Drag-and-drop: Select the element in the Form area and drag it to where you want in the questionnaire.
2. Move buttons: Select the element you want to move and use the move up/move down buttons in the Toolbar on top of the Form Area to move it up or down.
3. Cut-and-paste: Cut the element you want to move and use the drag-and-drop feature to move the placeholder to where you want to put it.
