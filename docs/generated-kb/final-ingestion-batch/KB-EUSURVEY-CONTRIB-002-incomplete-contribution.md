# What does "incomplete contribution" mean in EUSurvey?

## Intent / Description

Explains the meaning of an "incomplete" or "draft" contribution and why it appears in the contribution list.

## Applies To

* Role(s): Survey Manager, Respondent
* Feature: Contributions, Drafts
* Context: Viewing contributions or wondering why a response appears incomplete

## Short Answer

An "incomplete contribution" (also called a "draft") means a respondent started filling in the survey but did **not** click the final Submit button. Their answers are saved as a draft but not counted as a submitted contribution. The respondent can return later to complete and submit the form.

## Steps / Procedure

**For survey managers:**
1. Go to the **Results** page of your survey.
2. Drafts (incomplete contributions) are shown separately from submitted contributions.
3. You can filter to view only drafts or only submitted contributions.
4. You can export draft answers separately if needed.

**For respondents:**
1. If you saved your progress but did not click "Submit", your contribution is saved as a draft.
2. Return to the survey using the same link (or your draft link if provided).
3. Complete any remaining required questions.
4. Click **Submit** to finalize your contribution.

## Important Conditions / Limitations

* Draft contributions are **not** included in the official submitted contribution count.
* A draft is only accessible to the respondent who created it (via their draft link or authenticated session).
* The survey manager can view draft answers but they are clearly marked as drafts.
* If the survey closes (end date passes) before the respondent submits, the draft remains but cannot be submitted anymore.
* Drafts are saved when a respondent navigates between pages of a multi-page survey or clicks "Save as Draft".
* Browser local storage may also temporarily save answers as a backup, but the official draft is server-side.

## Troubleshooting / Related Cases

* If a respondent says they submitted but the contribution shows as draft, they likely clicked "Save as Draft" or closed the browser before completing the final Submit step.
* If many contributions are drafts, check whether mandatory questions or technical issues are preventing submission.
* Survey managers can export draft contributions separately for analysis.
