## Short Answer

Visibility settings let you show or hide elements depending on answers given to earlier single choice, multiple choice or matrix questions. This keeps the participant experience relevant and avoids unnecessary questions.

* Elements with dependencies remain hidden to participants until at least one configured triggering answer is selected.
