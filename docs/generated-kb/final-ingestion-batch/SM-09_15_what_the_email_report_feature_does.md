## Short Answer

The Email Report feature allows survey managers to receive automated reports on the number of contributions received by a survey. Reports can be sent daily, weekly or monthly to a designated list of recipients.

## Important Conditions / Limitations

* Reports are sent only while the survey is published.
* Reports stop automatically if the survey is unpublished, archived, frozen or deleted.
* Reports can be sent only when at least one contribution was received during the selected period.
