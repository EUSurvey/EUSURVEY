## Short Answer

Automatic scripts can falsify the outcome of an online survey by submitting a high number of contributions. To prevent this, you can use EUSurvey to make participants solve a CAPTCHA (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CAPTCHA) challenge before submitting a contribution.

## Prerequisites / Required Permissions

Not specified in source document.

## Procedure

1. You can enable/disable the CAPTCHA in the 'Security' settings under 'Properties'.
2. N.B. Although this won't make fraud impossible, it might discourage people from continuing to try to falsify survey results.
