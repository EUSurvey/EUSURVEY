## Short Answer

The 'Motivation popup' is a dialog window which opens to the survey participant during the completion of the form. It displays a message to motivate the participant to continue his progress. This message is customisable as well as the time at which the popup is displayed.

## Procedure

1. 'Motivation popup' is available in the survey's 'Properties' under the 'Appearance' tab.
2. Motivation popup in the survey's 'Properties' Once the switch has been activated, the options appear.
3. The 'Trigger' can be based on the progress or a timer.
4. 'Progress' is expressed as a percentage. For instance, 50%. This means that the popup will be displayed as soon as the survey participant answered 50% of the questions.
5. In case the 'timer' option has been selected, the popup will be displayed after X minutes. X being the number of minutes specified in the 'Threshold value' field.
6. Finally, the text is customizable using the 'Motivation text' field.
