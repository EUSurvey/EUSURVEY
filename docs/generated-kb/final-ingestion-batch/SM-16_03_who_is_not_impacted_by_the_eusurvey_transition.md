## Short Answer

Staff of EU Institutions, Bodies, Offices and Agencies are not affected. Users responding to surveys, participating in open public consultations, or accessing published results are also not affected. External users working on behalf of an EU institution may continue to manage surveys created by an EU institutional user if appropriate permissions are granted.
